# EXT-COMPLIANCE — organizational, privacy, and procurement assurance

Tracker: [#167](https://github.com/Bobcatsfan33/Pharos/issues/167).

GRC and Legal must validate applicable SOC 2/ISO evidence, control ownership, SIG/CAIQ and AI-CAIQ
answers, DPA, privacy, retention/deletion, litigation hold, residency, subprocessors, vulnerability
and incident obligations, insurance, accessibility, licensing, support, and procurement terms
against the real operating organization and customer boundary.

Approval requires the retained reviewed versions, exception/risk acceptances, and a signed decision
from named authorities distinct from the repository preparer. Templates and mappings are not an
attestation, completed questionnaire, legal opinion, or customer contract.
