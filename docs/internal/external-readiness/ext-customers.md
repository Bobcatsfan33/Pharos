# EXT-CUSTOMERS — design-partner and production validation

Tracker: [#168](https://github.com/Bobcatsfan33/Pharos/issues/168).

Product must run an approved pilot in the intended regulated-use boundary with an accountable
customer sponsor and predefined success criteria. Evidence must cover real workflow adoption,
governed actions and human review, efficacy and error handling, latency and operational burden,
recovery, incident, evidence export, support, known limitations, and customer risk acceptance.

Approval requires a signed design-partner or production reference identifying the product version,
deployment topology, dates, scope, limitations, results, and authorized customer representative.
Synthetic demos, internal dogfooding, and repository tests do not satisfy this gate.
